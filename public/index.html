About Soil Health Predictor

Soil Health Predictor helps farmers analyze soil health, detect nutrient deficiencies, and get clear fertilizer recommendations using real agronomic data and AI assistance.

Features

Everything a modern farmer needs, in one place.

Soil Nutrient Analysis

Get instant Low/Normal/High status for 12 key soil nutrients.

Soil Report OCR

Upload your lab soil report and we auto-fill the values for you.

Detailed Nutrient Report

Full breakdown of all 12 nutrients with fertilizer type, quantity and priority.

AI Farming Assistant

Ask AgriBot anything about fertilizers, soil health or irrigation, 24/7.

Live Weather

Real-time temperature, humidity and rainfall for your location.

Soil Health Score

A single 0-100 score showing your soil's overall health.
